Our Story

Three generations of flavour,
served with new-age care.

What began as a small family kitchen in Vidyanagar, Hubballi, has grown into one of the region's most trusted catering names — without ever losing the home-style care behind every dish. Today we plan and serve everything from intimate housewarmings to 2,000-guest wedding banquets, across Hubballi, Dharwad and the villages around them.

Got Questions?

Frequently asked questions

How much do you charge?
Pricing depends on guest count, menu and service style. Share your event details with us on WhatsApp or via the booking form below and we'll send a custom quote within hours.
Do you provide vegetarian catering?
Yes — we specialise in pure vegetarian menus across South Indian, North Indian and Chinese cuisines, including Jain options on request.
Can I customize the menu?
Absolutely. Every menu is built around your taste, budget and event type — mix and match dishes from any category.
Do you serve villages?
Yes, we cater across Hubballi, Dharwad and all surrounding villages within roughly 80 km, including Navalgund, Kalghatgi, Kundgol, Annigeri, Alnavar, Garag, Hebballi and Mugad.
How early should I book?
For weddings we recommend booking 2-3 months in advance. Smaller events like birthdays can usually be confirmed within a week, subject to availability.
Do you provide live counters?
Yes — dosa, chaat, pasta and dessert live counters are available and very popular at weddings and corporate events.
